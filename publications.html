The w-mode instability of strangeon ergostars and its impact on energy extraction

Haojia Xia, Hong-Bo Li, Enping Zhou, Ren-Xin Xu

论文摘要

Rapidly rotating compact ergostars have been proposed as alternative central engines for short gamma-ray bursts. However, competing energy-loss channels, such as gravitational-wave emission driven by non-axisymmetric instabilities, could curtail the power available for relativistic electromagnetic outflows. In this work, we investigate the w-mode instability of rapidly rotating strangeon ergostars by solving the coupled spacetime and fluid perturbation equations in the Hilbert gauge using a spectral method, without relying on a slow-rotation approximation. We calculate the complex eigenvalues of the axial-led and polar-led w-mode branches originating from the fundamental l = m = 2 modes of nonrotating stars along the maximum-energy-extraction paths of twelve representative strangeon equations of state. We find that both branches remain damped throughout these paths, despite the presence of ergoregions. We further vary the stellar angular velocity to determine the instability thresholds. In every case with an identified threshold, the angular velocity at the corresponding extraction-path endpoint remains below the critical value, with the largest ratio being 0.926. Our results indicate that the w-mode instabilities examined here do not provide a growing gravitational-wave emission channel along the considered energy-extraction paths, supporting the availability of the previously estimated rotational energy reservoirs for electromagnetic extraction.

期刊论文 2026 年 9 月 1 日

Strangeon ergostars

Haojia Xia, Shichuan Chen, Hong-Bo Li, Enping Zhou, Ren-Xin Xu

Journal of Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics · JCAP 09 (2026) 010

论文摘要

The nature of the central engine powering short gamma-ray bursts (sGRBs) in binary neutron star (BNS) mergers remains a key open question in the era of multi-messenger astronomy. The ergostar hypothesis, that a rapidly rotating star with an ergoregion can act as a powerful energy source, offers an alternative explanation to the black hole-accretion disk paradigm. In this work, however, we examine this hypothesis using a phenomenological EOS of strangeon matter, i.e., condensed matter with nucleon-like units for three flavors of quarks. By constructing a large suite of uniformly rotating equilibrium models, we systematically investigate the parameter space of the stable ergostars and calculate their maximum extractable energy. We demonstrate that strangeon matter supports a vast and robust parameter space for dynamically stable ergostars, even without requiring differential rotation. We find that the extractable rotational energy from these configurations can be on the order of 0.01M⊙, representing a massive energy reservoir, even when accounting for baryonic mass variations (e.g., mass ejection and particle capture during the Penrose process). Our results suggest that BNS merger remnants composed of exotic matter could play a crucial, previously underestimated role in high-energy astrophysics.
